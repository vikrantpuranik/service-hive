Information Collected

User Provided Information: You may provide Service Hive with "personally identifiable" information when you register, such as your name and e-mail address. Additionally, Service Hive provides the option to share demographic information, upload photographs, or provide other personally identifiable details. To become a member of the Site, you are required to provide information through the registration process, which may include your first name, last name, email address, zip code, gender, country, username, and password.

Additional demographic or background information may be requested, including but not limited to telephone number, marital status, education, occupation history, and non-personally identifiable information regarding household income or members. To ensure proper consent, you will be asked to confirm that you have obtained permission on behalf of any family or live-in members regarding their personally identifiable information.

You may edit your account information at any time by logging in and updating your preferences on the Dashboard; changes take effect immediately upon saving. Please keep this information current. Service Hive takes reasonable steps to ensure that collected data remains reliable, valid, complete, and relevant for its intended use.

"Automatically Collected" Information: When you visit the Site or open our HTML emails, we automatically collect information about your device and online activities. This "Response Metadata" (or "Response Data") is transferred and stored per this Privacy Policy and may be shared with survey generators, researchers, clients, and app developers. It may also be provided to unaffiliated third parties for their internal purposes, including marketing. You may opt-out of this sharing by contacting info@service-hive.pro or by deleting your account.

Response Metadata includes:

  • Cookies: Small text files sent to your computer to perform analytics, operate services, and assess usage trends. Service Hive uses both session and persistent cookies; the latter can be removed via your browser settings. Disabling cookies may cause Services to function improperly, and you assume responsibility for any diminished functionality.
  • Device Details: Technical specifications such as operating system, browser type, and IP address.
  • Geographic Location: Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Usage Metrics: Time, duration, and extent of service utilization.
  • Web Beacons: Small transparent graphic images used in emails to understand user behavior.

"Optionally Collected" Information: By agreeing to participate in contextual tracking, you grant us permission to passively collect behavioral data. This includes mobile device identifiers (like Apple's IDFA or Google's AAID) to match you with relevant survey opportunities. We may also record browsing history, search inputs, and application usage. You have the ability to pause or disable this tracking at any time.

Survey Information & User Feedback: When hosting surveys for our partners, Service Hive captures provided information, which is used in accordance with the partner's privacy practices. Any feedback or suggestions you provide to Service Hive regarding our Services may be utilized commercially by us without compensation to you.
